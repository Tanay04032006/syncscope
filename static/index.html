REFERENCE GUIDE
Choose the right IPC mechanism
Each technique makes a different trade-off between speed, isolation, structure, and reach.

        

          
┃
Pipes
A simple byte stream, ideal for related processes and one-way producer–consumer flows.
Strength
Simple & ordered
Scope
Local machine

          
≡
Message queues
Structured, buffered messages that decouple senders from receivers.
Strength
Clear boundaries
Scope
Local processes

          
▦
Shared memory
The fastest path for large data, with synchronization handled separately.
Strength
Zero-copy access
Risk
Race conditions

          
⌁
Sockets
Flexible communication that works locally and across a network.
Strength
Distributed reach
Scope
Local or remote
